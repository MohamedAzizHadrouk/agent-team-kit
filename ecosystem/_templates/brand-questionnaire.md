# Personal brand questionnaire – the owner

Used by `/wakil.brand --setup` (and by `/wakil.brand <section>`). Ask 3–5 questions at a time, in the owner's
language, each with a suggested default. "I don't know yet" → `TODO`. Never invent answers.
If PROFILE.md already answers something (languages, work, goals), show it as the default instead of asking again.

## A. Positioning (→ BRAND.md › Positioning)
1. In one sentence: who do you help, to get what result, and how?
2. Which 3–5 topics do you want to be known for?
3. What makes you different from others who do the same?
4. What should your brand bring you (clients, job offers, visibility, community, speaking…)? How will you measure it?

## B. Audience (→ BRAND.md › Audience)
5. Who exactly do you want to reach (roles, sectors, countries)? What do they struggle with?
6. In which languages do you publish?

## C. Story & proof (→ BRAND.md › Story & bios)
7. Your story in a few lines: where you come from, turning points, why you do this?
8. Which real projects, results or certificates may be mentioned publicly? Which clients / employers may be named?
9. Do you already have bios (LinkedIn, website)? Paste them – I'll keep or improve them.

## D. Voice (→ BRAND.md › Voice & tone)
10. Your personality in 3 words? Formal or friendly? Expert or storyteller?
11. Words or topics you never want to use / talk about publicly?

## E. Visual identity & assets (→ BRAND.md › Visual identity, assets/INDEX.md)
12. Colors, fonts, logo or monogram? Existing templates (Canva, Figma)?
13. Which photos of you may agents use (headshots, others)? Where are they?
14. CV, portfolio, website, videos – where do they live?

## F. Channels (→ one folder each: channels/<channel>/ via --add-channel, listed in channels/INDEX.md)
15. Which platforms are you on or want to be on (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn…)? Handle / link, active or planned?
16. For each: why you are there, who you reach, how often you want to post, which formats?

## G. Strategy (→ strategy/STRATEGY.md)
17. Main goal for the next 3–6 months, measurable targets, and where you are today?
18. Which 3–5 content pillars? Which channel matters most, and what role does each one play?
19. How many hours per week can you give to your brand? Batch days for creating / posting?

## G2. Content (→ channels/<channel>/CONTENT.md)
20. Any ideas already in mind, and for which channel?
21. What did you already publish that worked well (channel, link, result if you know it)?

## H. Privacy (→ BRAND.md › Privacy & likeness)
22. What may be public about you, and what must never be public?
