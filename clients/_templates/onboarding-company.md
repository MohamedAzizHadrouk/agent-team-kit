# Onboarding questionnaire – COMPANY (moral person)

Used by the project-lead. Ask in small groups (3–5 questions at a time), in the owner's language.
Accept "I don't know yet" → write `TODO`, add it to the project's Open questions. Never invent answers.

## A. Identity
1. Legal name and trade/brand name?
2. Country and legal form (SARL, SA, LLC…)? (Do not collect tax/registration numbers unless needed.)
3. Sector and what exactly they sell or offer?
4. Existing website and social channels?

## B. People
5. Who is the main contact (name, role, best channel)?
6. Who approves deliverables? One person or several?
7. Anyone else involved (IT, marketing, legal)?

## C. Business
8. Who is the target audience?
9. Who are the main competitors (or admired examples)?
10. What does success look like for this project (measurable if possible)?
11. Which languages and markets?

## D. Brand
12. Do they have a brand guide, logo files, colors, fonts? Where are they?
13. Tone of voice? Words to use or avoid?
14. Any existing Figma / design system?

## E. Constraints
15. Legal or regulatory constraints (regulated sector, claims restrictions, data protection)?
16. NDA or confidentiality level?
17. Things we must never do or say?

## F. First project (create PROJECT.md)
18. What is the project (website, app, UI/UX, marketing…)? Goal, scope, deadline?
19. Technical: existing repo, stack preferences, hosting, domain owner, who owns the accounts?
20. Which Trello board will be used? (Create one from board-spec.md if none.)

## After the answers
- Create `clients/<slug>/CLIENT.md` (template CLIENT-company.md) and `BRAND.md`, `projects/<project>/PROJECT.md`.
- Add the rows to `clients/registry.md`.
- Summarize to the owner what is filled and what is still TODO, and ask the missing items.
