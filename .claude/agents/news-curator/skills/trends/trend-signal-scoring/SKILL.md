# Trend signal scoring

## A trend is growth over weeks, not one news item
Score each candidate on what you can actually cite:
| Signal | Weak | Medium | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent sources | 1–2 | 3–4 | 5+ of different types (media, research, companies, communities) |
| Time | days | weeks | months, still growing |
| Hard evidence | opinions | launches, funding, job posts | adoption data, official reports, standards |

## Steps
1. Collect candidates from the day's searches in the preferred areas.
2. Score with the table; the overall strength is the lowest of the three unless clearly justified.
3. Compare with the recent TRENDS.md files: seen again → "Watching" (note the change); not seen for weeks → "Fading".
4. For each trend: what it is (2 lines), why it may matter to the owner (from preferences only), sources with dates.
5. Never invent numbers (search volumes, market sizes) – cite them or leave them out.
