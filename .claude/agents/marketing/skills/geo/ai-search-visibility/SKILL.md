# AI search visibility (GEO)

## What is established
- Google: AI Overviews and AI Mode build on the same index and ranking systems; **no special optimisation or markup
  is required** – a page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet. SEO fundamentals come first (`on-page-seo-audit`).
- Google's own guide says llms.txt does not help its AI features. Other answer engines may read it: optional, low
  cost, never a priority. Do not promise "GEO hacks" and never create inauthentic mentions.

## Steps
1. **Entity clarity**: one short, consistent definition of the client (who, what, where, for whom) used on the site,
   social profiles, directories and Google Business Profile. Same name, same facts everywhere.
2. **Answer-first pages**: list the real questions customers ask; answer each clearly in the first lines, then detail.
   Comparisons and FAQs only with true information.
3. **Evidence**: original data, real examples, named authors, cited sources – what makes a page worth citing.
4. **Structured data** that matches the visible content (Organization, Product, LocalBusiness, Article).
5. **Earned mentions**: real reviews, press, partners, communities – never fake or paid fake mentions.
6. **Measure**: Search Console (incl. its generative AI reporting where available), referral traffic from AI
   assistants, manual prompt checks on the main questions (date + prompt + result; no invented numbers).
