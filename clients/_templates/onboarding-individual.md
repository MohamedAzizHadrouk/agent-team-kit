# Onboarding questionnaire – INDIVIDUAL (physical person)

Used by the project-lead. Ask in small groups (3–5 questions at a time), in the owner's language.
Accept "I don't know yet" → write `TODO`. Never invent answers. Privacy first: collect only what the project needs.

## A. Identity
1. Name as it should appear publicly?
2. Profession or activity?
3. Is this project personal or professional (freelance / sole trader)?
4. Existing website or social accounts?

## B. Goals & audience
5. What do they want to achieve (get clients, visibility, sell something, find a job…)?
6. Who do they want to reach?
7. Which languages?

## C. Personal brand
8. Style, personality, values they want to show? Examples they like?
9. Do they have a logo, colors, photos, or nothing yet?
10. Voice: personal "I" (default) or something else?

## D. PRIVACY & CONSENT (mandatory before any content is made)
11. What information MAY be public (professional email, city, social links…)?
12. What must NEVER be public (home address, phone, ID, family, private photos…)?
13. May we use their photos / face / voice? Which ones exactly? (Default: no.) Consent recorded how and when?
14. Any legal pages or data-protection needs for their country? (Flag for legal review.)

## E. First project (create PROJECT.md)
15. What is the project (portfolio, personal site, branding, social presence…)? Scope and deadline?
16. Technical: existing site/repo, hosting, domain owner, who owns the accounts?
17. Which Trello board will be used? (Create one from board-spec.md if none.)

## After the answers
- Create `clients/<slug>/CLIENT.md` (template CLIENT-individual.md) and `BRAND.md`, `projects/<project>/PROJECT.md`.
- Add the rows to `clients/registry.md`.
- Summarize to the owner what is filled and what is still TODO, and ask the missing items.
