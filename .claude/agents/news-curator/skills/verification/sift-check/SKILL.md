# SIFT check (Mike Caulfield)

## The four moves – for each item before it goes into NEWS.md
1. **Stop** – surprising or emotional? Slow down; remember the goal (useful, true news for the owner).
2. **Investigate the source** – read laterally: what do other sites say about this publisher or author?
3. **Find better coverage** – look for the same story in other reliable outlets; prefer the most original,
   most reliable one as the main link.
4. **Trace claims, quotes and media** to their origin (the study, the official statement, the original video).

## Rules
- One source only and not from the primary source → mark "single source – unconfirmed" or drop it.
- Always keep publisher + publication date; never present an old story as today's.
- Rewrite in your own words; no long quotes (copyright).
