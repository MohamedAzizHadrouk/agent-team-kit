# Evidence-based learning plan

## Principles (rated "high utility" by Dunlosky et al., 2013)
- **Practice testing / retrieval** – recall before re-reading: quizzes, flashcards, explain without notes.
- **Distributed practice (spacing)** – short sessions spread over days and weeks beat cramming.
Also useful: **interleaving** related topics, and **deliberate practice** – a specific weakness, immediate feedback,
just beyond the current level. Re-reading and highlighting alone are low utility.

## Steps
1. Define the end goal as something the owner can **do** (a project, a test, a task at work).
2. Split into 3–6 milestones; each ends with a proof (mini-project, exercise set, quiz score).
3. Weekly rhythm from the time available: new material → retrieval the next day → review after ~1 week → ~1 month.
4. Every milestone gets a practice project (`/wakil.skill-project`) and a short self-test.
5. Progress review: what can be done now without help, what is still weak, adjust the plan.

## Sources
- Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan & Willingham (2013), *Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning

- Ericsson, Krampe & Tesch-Römer (1993), deliberate practice – https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.100.3.363
