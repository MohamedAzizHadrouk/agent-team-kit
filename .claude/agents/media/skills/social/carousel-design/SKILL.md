# Carousel design

## Structure
1. **Slide 1 – cover**: big promise or question, 6–10 words, strong contrast; a visual cue to swipe.
2. **Slides 2…n-1 – one idea per slide**: title + max ~25 words or one visual; same grid and margins on every slide.
3. **Last slide – CTA**: save / share / comment / link in bio, plus brand sign-off.

## Steps
1. Build a master template (grid, type scale, colours, logo spot, page counter) from BRAND.md; then fill slides.
2. Sizes: Instagram portrait 1080×1350 (4:5) is the usual choice; LinkedIn documents are uploaded as PDF –
   **verify the current specs on the platform's help pages before export** and note the date.
3. Readability: minimum ~28 px body text at 1080 px wide, contrast per WCAG AA, nothing important in the outer margins.
4. Export PNG/JPG (Instagram) or one PDF (LinkedIn); name files `<client>-<topic>-<nn>.png`.
5. Alt text for each slide in the usage notes.
